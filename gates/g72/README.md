# G72: the ported power pipeline reproduces the real-activity die maps

Registration and Amendment 1: `registration.md`.

## Run
```bash
gates/g72/run.sh main opentsense:g71dev            # build dir defaults to ../../_build/asap7 (routed ORFS designs)
```
Steps:
1. Copy the pristine ASAP7 cell Verilog out of the pinned ORFS image.
2. In the testbed image, patch the models (`opentsense/power/asap7_models.py`), compile both netlists with Icarus, and run all VCD windows in parallel (`sim.py`).
3. Run OpenSTA at TT in the ORFS image (`opentsense/power/vcd_power.tcl`), one job at a time.
4. Evaluate in the testbed image (`evaluate.py`).

Outputs go to `runs/<id>/`: `result.json`, `maps.npz` (32 × 32 W per tile, row 0 at the die's lower edge), `sim.json`, `manifest.json` (with the sha256 of every input), `power/*.txt`, `timing.json` and `run.log`. The VCDs are about 0.9 GB in total.

Inputs:
- **ibex programs:** `inputs/ibex_hex`, built from `inputs/ibex_sw` with RISC-V GCC 14.2 in WSL. The compiler is not in the image.
- **TPU stimulus:** regenerated by `opentsense/power/workloads.py` (seed 36). It is identical to G36's.
- **Routed designs:** the exploratory ORFS results, hashed in the manifest.

Notes:
- **Bit-for-bit reproduction:**
  - Every instance's power in all 10 reports equals the exploratory G34/G36 report exactly (max difference 0 W over 49,684 ibex and 102,251 TPU instances).
  - The ibex maps equal `g34_maps.npz` exactly.
  - So the port reproduces the pipeline itself, not just its totals.
- **Where the tile gap comes from:** the remaining tile-sum gap is OpenSTA's own. Its per-instance powers sum to slightly less than its design total; the most is 0.029%, on the clock-dominated idle TPU. The two unlocated ibex entries are hierarchical adder modules; their leaf cells are listed and placed separately.
- **Fix after the run:** `result.json` stores criterion (d) as `1.0` (a numpy bool serialised as float). `evaluate.py` was then changed to write a plain bool; the numbers are unaffected.
- **Plumbing run:** `runs/dev` ran only the two idle windows, before the amendment was fixed.
