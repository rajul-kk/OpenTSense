# G72 registration (copied from fyp/todo-gates.md after the run)

### G72: The ported power pipeline reproduces the real-activity die maps (O1, RQ1) · REGISTERED
- **Why:** ports the G30, G34 and G36 pipeline into the package.
- **Set-up:**
  - ASAP7 ibex (900 ps) and ASAP7 TPU (200 ps). Reuse the routed ORFS databases in `_build/asap7`; rerouting is not part of the gate.
  - Gate-level Icarus simulation of the programs → VCD windows → OpenSTA per-instance power → per-tile maps.
- **Metrics:** program checksums, activity annotation %, power density compared with the recorded values, and tile-sum conservation.
- **Pass if all hold:**
  - every program checksum matches the host reference;
  - annotation ≥ 99%;
  - power density within 3% of G34 (1.27 W/mm²) and G36 (8.9 W/mm²);
  - tile maps sum to the total within 0.1%.
- **A fail means:** the port is broken or the earlier numbers were fragile. Either way, record it.
- **Effort:** 3 days · **Output:** `gates/g72/`
- **Amendment 1 (1 Nov 2026, before the run):** how the bars are measured; no bar is loosened.
  - **Pipeline:** `gates/g72/run.sh`.
    - Pristine ASAP7 cell Verilog comes from the pinned ORFS image; it is byte-identical to the exploratory copy. `opentsense/power/asap7_models.py` applies the functional patch (and the LVT/SLVT renames); it reproduces the exploratory patched models exactly, apart from blank lines.
    - Icarus runs in the testbed image, and OpenSTA (`opentsense/power/vcd_power.tcl`) in the pinned ORFS image at TT.
    - The ibex programs are committed in `gates/g72/inputs/`, with their source; the RISC-V compiler is not in the image. The TPU stimulus is regenerated by `opentsense/power/workloads.py` and is identical to G36's.
    - The ibex simulations stop at 30,000 cycles rather than 80,000. All kernels finish by cycle 27,295, and the VCD window (cycles 1500 to 2500) is unchanged.
  - **(a) Checksums:** ibex kernels 0 to 3 report `done` and the host-reference checksum. TPU W1 and W2 each produce 616 result rows (as in G36), all equal to the reference; idle W3 produces none.
  - **(b) Annotation:** ≥ 99% of pins in each of the 8 VCD windows.
  - **(c) Density:**
    - the ibex compute mean (matmul, crc32, sort) is within 3% of 1.27 W/mm²;
    - TPU W1 is within 3% of 8.9 W/mm².
    - Both are measured at TT, with total power divided by the routed die area.
  - **(d) Tile sums:** each 32 × 32 map (8 windows, plus default activity for each design) sums to within 0.1% of OpenSTA's design total.
    - Instances are binned by their centre. Hierarchical module instances are skipped, because their leaf cells are listed and placed separately.
    - OpenSTA's totals are printed to 8 digits.
  - **Plumbing check (not the gate):** `runs/dev` ran only the two idle windows, ibex sleep and TPU W3. Every instance's power equalled the exploratory report exactly. W3's per-instance powers sum to 0.029% below OpenSTA's total. The gate run starts from scratch.
