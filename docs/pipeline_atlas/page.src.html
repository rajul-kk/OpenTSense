STAGE 6 · ELECTRO-THERMAL LOOP

Leakage feedback, and where it runs away

Leakage rises with temperature, which raises temperature. The loop's spectral radius ρ predicts the amplification 1/(1 − ρ) while ρ < 1; above 1 the loop has no fixed point. Points are measured cases; the line is the prediction.
